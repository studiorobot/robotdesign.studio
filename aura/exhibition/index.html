An interdisciplinary exhibition exploring creativity, authorship, and authenticity in human–robot collaboration. Featuring original paintings created by professional artists working alongside either another human artist or a robotic arm, the exhibition invites visitors to question their assumptions about artistic creation and the role of technology in art. Through artworks, artists’ reflections, and an interactive robotic installation, “the robot is not an artist” offers a unique perspective on the relationship between humans, machines, and the creative process.

live demo

An interactive installation in which a robotic arm paints in response to human attention. A visitor wearing eye-tracking glasses becomes the robot’s creative partner. Wherever their gaze settles on the eight-foot canvas, the robot follows in real time to paint alongside them. At the same time, other visitors are invited to paint elsewhere on the canvas, and the color they use most determines the robot’s next choice. Through this shared act of making, the installation explores how attention and influence shape the creative process, and who, in the end, can claim authorship.
